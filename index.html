Who we are

A Canadian supplier built around your project schedule.

Woose Solutions started in 2020 in the Greater Toronto Area with a simple idea: give builders one place to source the finishing materials a project needs. Today we supply lighting, faucets, sanitary ware, doors, WPC decking and flooring to developers, builders, contractors and distributors across Canada.
