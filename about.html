Our story

It started with one warehouse unit in North York.

In 2020, Woose Solutions opened with a narrow focus: supply the finishing materials that Toronto builders kept struggling to source together — lighting from one place, faucets from another, doors from a third.

Consolidating those categories under one supplier turned out to matter more than we expected. A single purchase order, a single delivery schedule and a single person to call removed a layer of coordination from every project. Five years on, we supply six product categories to developers, builders, contractors and distributors across Canada.

What we do

Six categories, held to one specification standard.

Whether a project needs a container of flooring or twenty bathroom faucets, the process is the same: confirm the specification, quote it properly, check it before it ships.

Lighting

Recessed downlights, pendants, vanity and commercial fixtures in LED.

Plumbing

Kitchen and bathroom faucets, toilets, tubs and matching fittings.

Doors

Interior and exterior doors with frames, hardware and trim.

Decking & fencing

WPC composite boards and fence panels rated for Canadian winters.

Flooring

SPC, laminate and engineered flooring for residential and commercial use.

Carpet

Broadloom and carpet tile for offices, corridors and rental units.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend.

01

Specification before price

We confirm what the product actually is — dimensions, finish, rating — before quoting. A cheap price on the wrong spec costs more later.

02

One person to call

Every project has a named contact who owns the order from enquiry to delivery. No ticket queue.

03

Checked before it ships

Quantities and finishes are verified before goods leave the warehouse. Problems get caught here, not on site.

04

Delivered on your schedule

We schedule around your framing and finishing dates, not around a fixed truck departure.

Next step
